01Overview

diddlersFreq0ff (the "App") is developed and published by an independent developer ("we," "us"). 02What's stored, and where

The App keeps a single small save file using your device's on‑device app storage (the same mechanism a website uses to remember a shopping cart, technically localStorage inside the App's embedded browser view). It contains only:

  • Your virtual coin balance, level, and experience points
  • The date you last claimed your daily reward

None of this is linked to your identity, none of it is sent to us or to anyone else, and none of it leaves your device unless you export or clear it yourself. Uninstalling the App deletes it.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or any government or financial identifiers. The App has no login, no user profiles, and no server component for us to store anything on even if we wanted to.

Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026 App version 1.0.0 Developer: DowntownDiddlerrr (Diddlerrr)

Card PreGrade is a free app that checks a trading card's centering, corners, edges and surface from photos you take. This page explains, in plain terms, what happens to your information. In short: your card photos are analysed on your phone and are never uploaded.

01Overview

Card PreGrade (the "App") is developed and published by an independent developer, DowntownDiddlerrr (also known as Diddlerrr; "we," "us"). The App has no account or login and shows no ads. It doesn't ask for your name, email or any other personal details. The condition estimate it shows is produced entirely on your device. It is not an official grade, and the App is not affiliated with any grading company.

02Camera & photos

Your card photos never leave your device. The live camera view and any photo you pick from your gallery are processed in memory by the App itself. Nothing is uploaded to us or to any server, and no cloud or AI service is involved.

The App uses the camera only while the scan screen is open, to find the card's edges and take the photo you capture. If you choose a gallery photo instead, the App reads only that one image.

03What's stored, and where

To show your recent scans, the App saves a small history in on‑device app storage (technically localStorage inside the App's embedded browser view). It contains at most 24 entries, each with:

  • A small, reduced‑size thumbnail of the scanned card
  • The measurements and estimated grade range from that scan
  • The date of the scan

This history is never sent anywhere. You can delete it at any time with Clear history on the App's About screen, by clearing the App's storage in Android Settings, or by uninstalling the App.

04Crash reports & usage stats

To fix bugs and understand which features are used, the App sends limited, anonymous technical data to two service providers:

  • Sentry (crash reporting): error messages and stack traces, the app version, and basic device and browser details (user agent). Email addresses are stripped from error text, and no user identity is attached.
  • PostHog (anonymous usage statistics): screen views and two events: that a card side was analysed (with which side and how long it took) and that a scan finished (with the estimated grade range and whether a back photo was used). A random anonymous ID is used, with no person profile. Autocapture of taps and typed text is off, and session recording is off.

Images, thumbnails and card details are never included in this data. If your device sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, the App does not load either service.

05Information we don't collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, card photos, or any government or financial identifiers. The App has no login, no user profiles and no ads, and we do not sell or share data for advertising.

06App permissions

  • CAMERA: lets the App show the live viewfinder and take the card photo. Android asks for your permission first, and you can decline and use gallery photos instead.
  • INTERNET: used only to send the anonymous crash reports and usage stats described above. Card analysis works without a connection.

07Children's privacy

The App is a general‑audience hobby tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the App collects no personal information from anyone.

08Your choices

You can clear your scan history in the App at any time, revoke the camera permission in Android Settings, or uninstall the App to remove everything it stored. To stop the anonymous diagnostics, enable Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control where your device supports it, or keep the App offline.

09Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the revised version at this same address and update the effective date above. Material changes, for example if the App ever began uploading images, will be called out clearly rather than folded in silently.

Preview build

This hosted preview can't reach outside services, so satellite tiles, live weather and auto-finding the water near you are unavailable here — the app says so wherever it needs them. Everything computed on the device works: the solunar day planner, the guide's calls, read-the-water diagrams, catch log, trophy room, trips, gear, patterns, water temperature, GPX export and logbook backup. Name a water by hand to get going.

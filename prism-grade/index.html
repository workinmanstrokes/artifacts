How an inspection works

  1. White light — reads surface finish and print quality.
  2. Raking light — a grazing angle that reveals texture, wear and scratches.
  3. UV light — checks for trimming, recoloring or other alteration.

Prism Grade estimates are generated by AI vision from the photos you provide. They are not a submission to, and are not affiliated with, PSA, BGS, CGC or SGC — treat the grade and value as a starting point, not an appraisal. Verify condition and recent comps before buying, selling or insuring a card.
